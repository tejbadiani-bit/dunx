The idea

The highlight, every possession.

Basketball's best moments are the dunk, the no-look pass and the rebound off the glass. Most people who love the game rarely get to feel them. DUNX rebuilds the court around those moments.

Pillar one
DUNK.

A lower rim puts the game's biggest moment within reach of everyday players. It's still earned, and now it's possible.

Pillar two
RALLY.

Up to three steps, then move it. Possessions flow like rallies: fast, continuous and social.

Pillar three
REBOUND.

The walls are live. Bank it past your defender, play a give-and-go with the glass, set up the finish.

The precedent

Padel went from 15,000 to 860,000 UK players in six years by rebuilding a much-loved sport around a smaller, social, glass court. DUNX does the same for basketball.

How it plays

Learn it in five minutes.

2v2 · 3v3

Small teams mean everyone touches the ball on every possession.

Three steps

Carry it up to three steps, then pass, shoot, dunk or play the wall.

Live walls

The glass is part of the court. Bank passes, wall assists and give-and-gos.

1 or 2

One point inside the glowing arc, two from beyond it. That's the whole system.

First to 15

Win by two. Games take 10–15 minutes, so you fit several into every booking.

Safe landings

Protected landing zones, breakaway rims, no undercutting and no hanging on the rim.

The court

A glass box that glows.

Compact, enclosed and lit from the floor up. A DUNX court with its run-off takes 96 m², so six fit in the space of three padel courts.
